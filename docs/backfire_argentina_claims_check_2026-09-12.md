# "Backfire" claim check: the Argentina rescue story (Feb 2025)

Requested overnight ("poke holes in it, you have the internet") while checking
the Argentina mentions flagged earlier (scenes 764–806, 03:17:55–03:21:02).
Independent research via web search, not just the video itself — findings
below, sourced, not asserted as settled.

## What Ballard says in the video

Reconstructed from the master transcript, 03:17:55 onward: he says he was
"working with the police in Argentina" in February 2025 and "rescued 20
children [garbled — possibly "20 children, five years old," unclear],"
found digital evidence of "about 200 other kids," didn't have the hardware
to process it fast enough, went back to the US to get equipment, and
couldn't return because of "the Doug Anderson lie from the PR committee."
He also says "that's the first house we hit in **La Plata**." A second,
shorter retelling around 03:34:43–03:35:05 repeats the same 20-kids/200-videos
figures.

## What independent Argentine reporting actually documents

A real operation did happen, in the right window, and Ballard's involvement
is independently confirmed — this part isn't in dispute:

- **Timing checks out.** Ballard met Javier Milei at CPAC, then two days
  later accompanied Argentina's Federal Police (PFA) and Public Prosecutor's
  Office (MPF) on raids in **Mar del Plata, Florencio Varela, and La
  Matanza** — mid-to-late February 2025, matching his "February of 2025"
  claim.
- **The hardware complaint checks out, closely.** Local outlet *La Capital*
  (Mar del Plata), Feb 25, 2025, quotes Ballard directly at the scene:
  police "haven't been able to extract photos from the computer, videos"
  within an hour of access, and him saying "the problem is resources, not
  heart... what they lack are necessary government budgets." That's
  essentially the same complaint he repeats in the video, contemporaneous
  and on the record — this specific detail holds up.
- **The specific numbers don't match, in every source found.** Multiple
  independent Argentine outlets reporting on the Mar del Plata raid
  specifically — the one operation with real operational detail available —
  describe **one 23-year-old suspect arrested** for producing child sexual
  abuse material at a residence near a kindergarten, and **two girls, ages
  5 and 9 (the suspect's nieces), rescued**. Devices with roughly 170GB of
  content, children's clothing, and toys were seized. No source found
  mentions 20 children rescued, or 200 additional children identified, in
  Argentina in February 2025.
- **"La Plata" doesn't appear anywhere.** Every source found names **Mar
  del Plata** (a distinct coastal city, ~400km from La Plata, the actual
  city named "La Plata," capital of Buenos Aires province). No source
  mentions a raid in La Plata itself. Could be Ballard misspeaking,
  conflating the two similarly-named cities in a 19-months-later retelling,
  or genuinely a separate incident nothing has been found on — not resolved
  either way.

## What this doesn't establish

- Not proof the "20 kids / 200 videos" figures are invented — he could be
  aggregating multiple operations across the three cities into one number,
  or citing a private/unpublished count OUR itself has that never made
  Argentine press. No source found confirms that aggregate figure, but
  absence of a source isn't proof of absence.
- Not a claim that the underlying event didn't happen — it clearly did, is
  independently documented, and Ballard's presence and specific complaint
  about equipment are corroborated by a contemporaneous, on-the-record
  quote.
- The Doug Anderson / "PR committee blocked my return" claim is entirely
  self-sourced — nothing independent found addresses it either way.

## Separately confirmed: the Elder Cook / Tabernacle Choir claim checks out

Ballard's other Argentina-adjacent claim in this same passage — "just months
after... Elder Cook leads an enormous delegation with the Mormon Tabernacle
Choir into Argentina" — is independently confirmed and accurately timed:
the Tabernacle Choir's "Songs of Hope" tour stopped in Buenos Aires Aug
17–24, 2025 (Church centennial in South America), with Elder Quentin L.
Cook accompanying and meeting President Milei on Aug 20, 2025. Six months
after the February operation — "just months after" holds up as stated.
(Church News, KSL, LDS Newsroom — see Church News Aug 20/28 2025 coverage.)
