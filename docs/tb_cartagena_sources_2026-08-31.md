## Does this resolve the "convicts currently free" question?

**Not fully, but it reframes it.** No source found says the four/five
Cartagena defendants are currently free as of the Oct 2024 conviction —
that specific claim from the Whisper transcript remains unconfirmed across
all 13 sources checked this session (11 here + 2 fetched earlier). What
*is* independently confirmed, by two separate sources (El Tiempo 2023,
Nación Paisa), is a **real, earlier** vencimiento-de-términos release —
in 2016, two years after the 2014 operation, well before the 2024
conviction/appeal. The sources disagree on scope: El Tiempo says everyone
captured was freed then; Nación Paisa names only Suárez Moya. Most likely
read: the Whisper transcript's narrator conflated this real 2016 pretrial
release with the 2024 appeal outcome, producing a "convicted on appeal but
still free" framing that doesn't match any single source. **Still not
usable in wiki text as originally framed.**

## Other genuinely new findings

- **A 5th defendant, Horacio Revollo/Revolledo Pacheco**, appears in the
  two 2023-era sources (El Tiempo, Nación Paisa) but not the four Oct-2024
  sources, which mention "one acquitted" without naming them. Combined,
  this resolves cleanly: 5 people prosecuted, 4 convicted/sentenced to 16
  years each, 1 (almost certainly Revollo/Revolledo Pacheco) acquitted.
- **Minors-rescued count has a real discrepancy**: 54 (Noticias Caracol,
  El País, Razón+Fe, Nación Paisa — the majority) vs. "60 jóvenes,
  incluidas 25 menores" (El Tiempo 2023). Worth noting both if this is ever
  cited rather than picking one uncritically.
- Ballard's pre-Colombia agency is inconsistently reported: FBI (most
  sources) vs. "U.S. Department of Homeland Security" (Infobae). Another
  discrepancy worth flagging rather than resolving by picking whichever
  sounds more familiar.
