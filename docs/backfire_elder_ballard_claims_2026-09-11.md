# "Backfire" — claims about "Elder Ballard" (M. Russell Ballard)

Every claim in the video about the other Ballard — identified at
00:20:24 as "Russell Ballard, who died as the president of the [Quorum]
of the Twelve Apostles" (real person: M. Russell Ballard, LDS apostle
and President of the Quorum of the Twelve, died November 2023; no
stated relation to Tim Ballard beyond shared surname). Whisper renders
his name inconsistently — "Elder Ballard" most often, but also "Elder
Bellard" and, once, "President Emmerlund Bellard." Timestamps are exact
cue-start times in the full video (source: `youtube__pBo_2DYxfrY_
master_stitched_patched.srt`).

## A — Identity and closeness of the relationship

- **00:14:34** — First mention: calls him "my best friend, President
  Emmerlund Bellard," says he was "betrayed" alongside him.
- **00:20:24** — Full identification: "Russell Ballard, who died as the
  president of the [Quorum] of the Twelve Apostles. He was my best
  friend... like a grandpa to me."
- **03:29:53** — Extends the framing beyond just the two of them: calls
  "the Ballard family" his "best friends" too, in a rhetorical question
  about whether it's plausible they'd be cut off.

## B — Disputing the Church's own account (core contentious cluster)

The Church's public statement (per the article's existing sourcing,
independent of this video) is that Elder Ballard's association with
Tim Ballard ended. This video directly and repeatedly disputes that:

- **00:21:26** — Claims Elder Ballard "was calling me" weeks before
  people at the Church would claim he'd cut Ballard off.
- **00:21:44** — Calls the Church's statement "horrible, horrible, evil
  defamatory" for saying Elder Ballard cut him off "for having used his
  name to do immoral things."
- **00:22:43** — Repeats: "Elder Ballard was calling me in August of
  2023, having not cut me off. Still my best friend."
- **00:29:55** — Claims a third party (name unclear in transcript,
  possibly "Russell Brunson" — needs a direct listen) independently
  confirmed "that statement's a lie because Elder Ballard called me."
- **02:06:55** — The strongest version of this claim: says there is "a
  phone call documenting" that Elder Ballard did not cut him off,
  directly contradicting what he says the Church/PR side would claim.
  **No such recording has been produced or referenced anywhere else in
  this project's research as of this doc** — flagged as an assertion of
  evidence that exists, not evidence itself.
- **02:32:44** — Reiterates the "lie" framing again, tied to the
  original tabloid-magazine story.

## C — "He recruited me, not the other way around"

- **00:30:08** — Claims it was Elder Ballard, not himself, who
  initiated the connection to Operation Underground Railroad: "the way
  that I found out about Tim Ballard and O.U.R. was actually from Elder
  Ballard. He personally called me and asked me to help."
- **00:30:35** — Restates the direction of initiative: "Elder Ballard
  and I tried get me in. It was the opposite way around" — i.e., he
  says he didn't seek the association out.

## D — Institutional-association claims (used as legitimacy evidence)

- **00:29:07** — Says he "worked with Elder Bednar" (a separate,
  real LDS apostle, Elder David A. Bednar), consulting on "the
  dedicated prayer" for an unnamed project.
- **00:29:18** — Claims he provided security for Elder Bednar and Elder
  Ballard "at the request of the Security Department" — offered as
  evidence his church ties were official/sanctioned, not self-invented.
- **02:06:32** — Claims Elder Ballard "personally told us that he set
  apart my son, my oldest son. He personally chose [him]" — a specific,
  named-family claim about a direct ecclesiastical act.

## E — Defense against a "misused his name" allegation

- **03:44:19 / 03:44:32** — Responds to what he attributes to Salt Lake
  Tribune reporting: "Tim has never misused Elder Ballard's name...
  where is the proof?" Says he's "been with Tim since 2013" (unclear
  antecedent for "Tim" here relative to Ballard's own first-person
  narration elsewhere — needs a direct listen, this passage reads oddly
  in third person about himself).

## Caveats

- All of section B rests entirely on Ballard's own account. The
  claimed phone-call recording (02:06:55) in particular is an assertion
  that evidence exists, not evidence itself — nothing to verify it
  against as of this doc.
- Several proper nouns/names in this cluster are Whisper garbles not
  yet confirmed against the actual audio (the "Russell Brunson" name at
  00:29:55, the third-person "Tim" reference at 03:44:19).
- Not a fact-check of whether Elder Ballard actually cut off contact —
  a structured record of what's claimed, same purpose as the other docs
  in this set.
