# "Backfire" — truth claims inventory

Same tagging convention as the earlier Fearless Brothers claims
inventory: **[F]** independently verifiable fact (dates, dollar
figures, court records, published articles), **[T]** third-party/
hearsay claim (attributed to someone else, "he told me," not
independently checkable from the video alone), **[S]** subjective/
spiritual framing (not fact-checkable by nature). Paraphrased, not
quoted at length — timestamps point to the master transcript for exact
wording. Source: `youtube__pBo_2DYxfrY_master_stitched_patched.srt`.

This is a first pass over the most checkable material (financial
figures, court outcomes, media conduct, dated events) — not exhaustive
of every claim in a 3h52m video.

  (00:21:11), later cited as over $250 million (00:23:19). Both

  timeline — worth checking against actual box-office reporting for
  the film, which is public record.
- **[F]** "100 million people" have seen the film (00:22:54, 00:23:24)
  — no source given for this figure; likely derived from streaming/
  distribution claims rather than box office alone, worth checking
  what it's based on.

  million dollars" on Ballard-related books, despite the church
  saying it had no association with him (00:28:41).
- **[T]** "$10 million... sent to destroy my life" — attributed to an
  unnamed third party ("she said" / "he said," transcript renders it
  both ways at 00:24:33 and 00:25:44), not sourced further.

## Caveats

- Several names are likely Whisper mis-transcriptions ("Todd
  Chahennancy," "Dagan," "Cooper") — none of these should be treated
  as confirmed spellings without checking the actual audio.
- This inventory intentionally skips purely rhetorical/spiritual
  passages (scripture citations, prayer, direct appeals to the
  audience) — those aren't truth claims in the sense this doc is
  tracking, even though they make up a large share of the runtime.
- Not a fact-check — a structured list of *what's claimed* and how
  checkable each claim is, for whoever wants to actually verify one.
