# "Backfire" — claims involving "the state president" and "deep state"

Companion to the blame index and speech-patterns docs. This one is about
substance, not pronunciation — what Ballard actually alleges about the
(unnamed) stake president, and separately, every time he invokes "deep
state" language. Same source/method as the other docs: full transcript,
chronological order, timestamps are full-video elapsed time.

## The (unnamed) stake president — claim threads

He is never named by Ballard in this video (see the blame index for
named parties — Doug Anderson, Kevin Pearson, Matt Holland). Distinct
claims made about him, roughly in the order they build across the video:

1. **Involved in orchestrating the excommunication**, alongside Kevin
   Pearson (area president) and the PR committee — 00:18:52, 00:31:37,
   00:34:11, 01:04:44.
2. **Allegedly instructed to lie / told he could override scripture** —
   Ballard addresses someone else directly: "did you tell the state
   president to lie?" and "you instructed the state president to lie and
   violate scriptures" (02:37:35–02:37:52). A separate claim: someone
   "instructed the state president that he is allowed to override the
   words of Jesus" (02:42:13).
3. **Blocked Ballard's defense** — allegedly didn't let him address the
   tabloid story (00:52:44), denied him a Matthew 18 process / witnesses
   (02:13:14, 02:13:29), and didn't reach out for "Tim's side of the
   story" before the statement went out (02:12:53–02:13:00).
4. **Told Ballard he could "delete scripture"** if it was inconvenient
   (00:58:45).
5. **Continues public hostility after the excommunication** — "takes
   every opportunity to bully me from the pulpit" (03:06:17), described
   as still commenting against him "regularly" as of "last week"
   (03:29:31, 03:30:22).
6. **Recently installed** — a claim that "the very state conference
   where the state president was recently called" is the same one
   referenced regarding a subsequent decision (02:43:18).
7. **More sympathetic/secondary claims**: the "first counselor" in the
   presidency is called "a good guy" Ballard says he forgives but
   doesn't trust (00:55:02); the state president's counselors are
   described as visibly upset/"crying" during a confrontation
   (00:46:39); Christa Casey is said to have "lied to the state
   president" (00:57:33) — see the named-accusers doc for more on her.
8. Ballard says at one point he's deliberately not naming him: "I'm not
   using the state president's name because I feel bad for him. He
   didn't have courage" (02:37:35).

## "Deep state" — 5 mentions, all in the church-governance sense

Not the conventional US-politics usage — every instance refers to an
alleged hidden layer of church bureaucracy/leadership acting outside
official channels, not federal government.

- 00:32:16 — worries "some deep state elements" within the church could
  leak the story publicly.
- 01:05:41 — "the deep state gets to have their way with the name of
  the prophet" (following a line about people invoking the prophet's
  name without authorization).
- 02:49:07 — accuses unnamed "bosses" / "the deep state" of "running
  cover" and lying about evidence in a headline.
- 03:30:51 — rhetorical: "is it groupthink and the deep state gets to
  decide?"
- 03:47:17 — most explicit: "I'm talking about a deep state. I'm
  talking about people who follow the order of the lukewarm." Directly
  ties the "deep state" framing to the "lukewarm" language covered in
  `backfire_speech_patterns_2026-09-11.md`.
