## 1. Kely Suarez is a convicted trafficker

![Kely Suarez Moya conviction](../inputs/tb_lawfare_screenshots/tb10.png)

Site's own words: "recruited a child sex trafficker named Kely Suarez Moya
to sue Tim, Angel Studios, and others for defamation... In 2014, Ms. Moya
was in fact arrested during a sting operation..."

**This claim is TRUE and independently confirmed** — but by a different,
separate case than the one this page is actually about. Her 2014 arrest and
2024 conviction (16-year sentence, upheld on appeal) is the "Operación
Cristal 2" Cartagena trafficking case, confirmed across 13 independent
Colombian/English news sources — see `tb_cartagena_sources_2026-08-31.md`.
**That trafficking conviction is not the same thing as the outcome of
*Suárez v. Angel Studios/Ballard*, the separate civil defamation suit she
filed against Ballard** — that case is still active and is the one under
the standing project guardrail ([[feedback_tim_ballard_wiki_legal_caution]]):
no conviction/ruling detail about *that* case goes into wiki material until
the Utah Supreme Court actually rules. Her trafficking conviction is a
different, already-decided matter and is fine to state as fact, sourced to
the Colombian press, not to Ballard's own framing of it.
