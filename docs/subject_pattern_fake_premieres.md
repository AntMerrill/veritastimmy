# Pattern: staged film releases with misrepresented box-office performance

Working notes on a recurring Tim Ballard M.O., per John (2026-08-27): stage
a theatrical release for a film, then publicly claim box-office results
that don't match what's actually reported — and repeat the release cycle
roughly every few months rather than letting a flop stand.

## Hidden War (2025) — the case in hand

- Reported worldwide gross ~US$4,561 (The Numbers) — domestic gross not
  reported at all. By any normal reading, a flop.
- Ballard's own account (Jade Warwick interview, 2025-12-20) frames the
  November 14, 2025 U.S. release as a "fake launch" — minimal publicity,
  deliberately structured (per Ballard) to "detect interference with
  theater bookings," with Utah singled out as the one state that didn't
  open that day. See `drafts/wikipedia/hiddenwar_mediaappearances_image_new.txt`
  for the sourced article text.
- The film's own standalone Wikipedia article (`Hidden War (film)`) did
  not survive AfD and was merged into the `Tim Ballard` article — see
  `docs/wiki_drafts.md`. Its promotional poster (`File:Hiddenwarpromo.jpg`)
  is now orphaned as a result (`{{Di-orphaned non-free use|date=21 August
  2026}}`, scheduled for deletion 2026-08-28 per WP:CSD#F5's 7-day clock).

## The claimed pattern

John's framing (2026-08-27, paraphrased from a live conversation, not yet
independently sourced beyond the Hidden War case above): Ballard
re-premieres/re-launches underperforming films on a recurring cycle —
roughly every 3 months — rather than letting a poor box-office result
stand as final. If true, this would mean Hidden War's 2025-11-14 "fake
launch" isn't a one-off but an instance of a repeatable playbook.

**Not yet verified**: no second Ballard film/re-release has been checked
against this cadence claim yet. Before this goes into any live wiki text,
it needs independent sourcing (a re-release date, box-office claim vs.
actual reported numbers, ideally for at least one other title) — per this
repo's existing standard for the Hidden War material itself (sourced to
The Numbers + Ballard's own on-record interview, not just asserted).

## Independent coverage search — 2026-08-27, came up empty

Searched for independent journalism on Hidden War specifically (to
support recreating the standalone article and to strengthen the box
office claim beyond The Numbers). Found:

- **Data aggregators only, no editorial content**: IMDb, Letterboxd, Box
  Office Mojo, Rotten Tomatoes (0 critic reviews, 0 audience ratings —
  zero reviews of any kind as of this check), Fandango (critic-reviews
  page exists but lists no actual critics).
- **Not independent**: `thegoodnewsroom.org`'s "Tim Ballard Discusses His
  Documentary 'Hidden War'" (2025-11-21, byline Fernanda Pierorazio) is
  the Archdiocese of New York's own outlet — a friendly Q&A platform, not
  arm's-length coverage.
- **Not journalism**: an ACCESSWIRE-syndicated piece on
  markets.financialcontent.com is a distributor press release, not
  editorial content.

**Useful non-journalism find**: Box Office Mojo's per-week breakdown is
more granular than the existing The Numbers citation — confirms two
distinct release events (a Nov 14 "fake launch" per Ballard, then a real
Nov 20 wide release, 53 theaters, $2,885 opening) and a documented 91.7%
week-2 decline (13 theaters, $239) into a near-total week-3 collapse (1
theater, $20). Worth adding as a second citation to the existing article
text, but it's a database, not "significant coverage" — doesn't clear
WP:NFILM notability on its own.

**Conclusion**: as of 2026-08-27, no independent news outlet has covered
Hidden War. Recreating the standalone `Hidden War (film)` article isn't
supportable yet on notability grounds. Worth re-checking periodically —
independent coverage sometimes lags a film's release, especially for a
title this obscure.
