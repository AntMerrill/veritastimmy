# Brazilian Senate testimony — "Canoa" ties back to the Ecuador clip

Checked the Brazilian Federal Senate CDH hearing (May 20, 2025, Ballard's
testimony, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-F9orJXMvR4) for references to
"Canoa"/"canoe" — following up on the Ecuador Facebook clip where Ballard
said "we are here in [Canoa/'Canola'], Ecuador" and called it "the house
depicted in Hidden War." Source: our own Whisper transcription
(`youtube__-F9orJXMvR4.srt`), Portuguese audio with English simultaneous
interpretation elsewhere in the hearing.

## What ties together

**Canoa is a real place, and this passage confirms it's in Ecuador.**
Canoa is an actual coastal town in Manabí province, Ecuador — and it was
significantly damaged by the real, independently documented **April 2016
Ecuador earthquake** (magnitude 7.8, epicenter near Canoa/Pedernales).
That's a verifiable historical event, not something invented for this
testimony.

**Key passage [00:39:48]:**

> "...que os pedófilos, holandês, eles vão atrás das pessoas vulneráveis.
> A mãe, ela é enganada e acaba sendo levada para esse hotel. E esse
> terremoto lá em Canoa."
>
> ("...the pedophiles, Dutch, they go after vulnerable people. The
> mother is deceived and ends up being taken to this hotel. And that
> earthquake there in Canoa.")

This directly links three elements found separately before:
- **"Canoa"** — same place name (Whisper-garbled as "Canola" in the
  Facebook clip)
- **"esse hotel"** ("this hotel") — same building/location language used
  for the house in the Facebook clip
- **Dutch suspects** — matches the Ecuadorian police officer's own
  testimony from the 2025 raid clip ("eram holandeses")

**Also confirms "Hidden War" [01:47:00]:**

> "...o fim da operação, todo mundo estava indo para casa... e esse é o
> fim de Hiddin War, do filme."
>
> ("...the end of the operation, everyone was going home... and that's
> the end of Hidden War, the film.")

Same project name Ballard used in the Facebook clip ("that is the house
depicted in Hidden War"), now also referenced in this separate,
much-earlier (May 2025) Senate testimony.

## The number and the explicit Ecuador confirmation

**[00:35:06–00:35:46]:**

> "...porque Deus viu 200 e 20 crianças prestes a serem brutalmente
> atacadas numa vila de canoa. Essa ilha de canoa me lembra de Marajó...
> Nós acabou que conseguimos resgatar essas crianças, essas 200 e poucas
> crianças... estamos falando de milhares, crianças da cana [Canoa?],
> sem tenas [unclear] no equador."
>
> ("...because God saw 220 children about to be brutally attacked in a
> canoe/Canoa village. This canoe/Canoa island reminds me of Marajó...
> we ended up managing to rescue those children, those 200-something
> children... we're talking about thousands, children from
> Canoa[?]... in Ecuador.")

**"no equador"** ("in Ecuador") is explicit and unambiguous here — this
whole passage is about Ecuador, removing any doubt about which country
"Canoa" refers to. The **220 children** figure is new and doesn't match
anything found in the Backfire video's own Argentina claims (20
children/200 videos) or the Ecuador raid officer's account (2 girls) —
a third, different number for what may or may not be the same underlying
story.

One more unresolved transcription oddity: **"crânia"** appears twice
("minha mulher mandou para o crânia," "mandou seu marido para o crânia")
— not a real Portuguese word. Could be yet another Whisper garble of
"Canoa," or something else entirely. Flagged, not resolved.

## Two more Canoa/canoe mentions, less clear

- **[00:35:46]:** "...quando nós chegamos a canoa nessa ilha, nós
  tivemos que cuidar deles. Então esses traficantes de Dau Landa
  [Holanda/Holland?] estavam dando tudo para eles..." — ambiguous
  between "when we arrived by canoe" and "when we arrived at Canoa";
  another possible "Dutch" (Holanda) reference, though garbled
  differently this time ("Dau Landa").
- **[00:37:44]:** "...essas crianças da Ukraine que foram resgatadas lá
  da guerra... no fim, elas vão ser mandadas para a canoa." ("...these
  children from Ukraine who were rescued from the war... in the end,
  they'll be sent to Canoa.") — a separate Ukraine-war-rescue storyline
  gets folded into the same Canoa narrative here. Not yet checked
  against anything else in this project.

## What this doesn't establish

- Not confirming the underlying rescue claims are true or false — this
  doc only establishes that the same location/hotel/nationality/project
  -branding elements recur across two separate, differently-formatted
  presentations (a casual Facebook clip and formal Senate testimony),
  months apart in production even though this testimony itself predates
  the Facebook clip (May 2025 testimony vs. September 2026 Facebook
  post).
- The 220-children figure is new and unreconciled against the other
  numbers already documented for Ecuador/Argentina claims elsewhere in
  this project (2 girls per Ecuadorian police, 20/200 in the Backfire
  video's own Argentina passage).
- "Crânia" and the Ukraine-children thread are both flagged, not
  resolved.
