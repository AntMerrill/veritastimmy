# "Backfire" — the "Kevin" problem: name munges and what's actually quotable

Prompted by the en.wikipedia.org "Tim Ballard" article's current Backfire section
ending in a broken, unattributed sentence: *"...like the 'hit job he got from his
state president Kevin."* Traced this back to the actual video
(`youtube__pBo_2DYxfrY`, "Backfire: The Excommunication Story of Tim Ballard,"
Official Tim Ballard channel, posted 2026-09-04) via our own corrected transcript
(`corrections/youtube__pBo_2DYxfrY_master_corrected.srt`).

**Bottom line: there are 3 distinct real people/roles named "Kevin" in this video,
not 23** — but they're three completely unrelated threads (two church-discipline
figures, one national-politics aside), which is why it reads as a running gag of
"which Kevin do you mean." Counts below are grep counts over the corrected
transcript, not the "23" figure floated in conversation — worth being precise
about this before anything goes near a wiki edit.

## The three Kevins

### 1. Kevin Pearson — LDS Area President (19 occurrences)

Ballard's claim: Pearson ordered/sent the person who carried out his
excommunication.

- 00:18:36 — "he was appalled at the workings of the area president, Kevin
  Pearson"
- 01:10:05 — "Kevin Pearson is being moved to Europe"
- 01:49:32 — "wasn't following Kevin Pearson [to] stick president" [sic, garbled
  tail]
- 02:41:56–02:42:45 — "The person who Kevin Pearson sent to order the
  excommunication... is a man named Kevin Hamilton... he's the one that Kevin
  Pearson purportedly ordered to come do the hit job"
- 03:23:56 — "like Kevin Pearson for bad or Jack Girard for good" (contrast —
  Girard/Jarard framed positively, not blamed)
- **Name munge found this pass**: 01:16:22 — "and that's what you did Kevin
  Pierce. That's what you—" — same person (direct-address "you did," matches
  the accusatory register used toward Pearson elsewhere), "Pierce" is almost
  certainly Whisper mishearing "Pearson." Not yet reflected as a corrected
  spelling anywhere in the existing backfire_*.md docs — worth a manual-SRT-
  correction pass entry.

### 2. Kevin Hamilton — genealogy department employee (6 occurrences)

Named by Ballard as the specific person Pearson allegedly ordered to execute
the excommunication action ("the hit job"), while working in the Church's
Genealogy Department, not the Area Authority — Ballard frames this as giving
Pearson "plausible deniability."

- 00:59:44 — "not to Kevin Hamilton's policy"
- 02:41:57–02:44:11 — the core passage: "The person who Kevin Pearson sent...
  is a man named Kevin Hamilton... has Kevin Hamilton ever expressed his
  opinions about this?... So Kevin Hamilton, he's the one that Kevin Pearson
  purportedly ordered to come do the hit job... he's not exactly in the area
  authority, he was working in the genealogy department"
- 02:43:34 — "...wife in the congregation and Kevin [Hamilton, from
  surrounding context] said he was really nice" (surname not repeated in this
  clip, but same thread)

### 3. Kevin McCartney [sic] — almost certainly Kevin McCarthy (1 occurrence)

**Completely unrelated to the excommunication story.** 00:23:44: "that Kevin
McCartney, the speaker of the House, had come to me and said, what can we—".
"Speaker of the House" makes this near-certainly a Whisper mishearing of
**Kevin McCarthy** (real-world former U.S. Speaker of the House), not a
genealogy-department or Church figure at all — likely phonetically pulled
toward "McCartney" (Paul McCartney is the far more common training-data name).
This is a real, highly public political figure, in a completely different
part of the video (a political anecdote), unconnected to Pearson/Hamilton.
Not previously logged in `docs/backfire_named_accusers_index.md` or either
non-dictionary-words doc — worth adding there separately.

## What's actually quotable for the Wikipedia fix

The current broken sentence sits in the "Documentation of release date"
subsection, where it doesn't even belong topically (that subsection is about
verifying the video's upload date, not about excommunication claims) — it
reads like a stray/unfinished edit that landed in the wrong place. The content
it's gesturing at is really part of the "Subsequent episodes and status"
material (fits the "Who Shot the First Bullet" episode description already
there).

**FINALIZED 2026-09-22, not yet posted to English wiki** (per John: fix
English later, Spanish gets this content first — see
`veritastimmy/drafts/wiki_edit_es_backfire_excommunication_2026-09-22.md`).
Accurate, attributable replacement (ABOUTSELF — Ballard's own claim, not
stated as fact, same standard as the rest of the Backfire section), reusing
the citation already defined in the article for this video (`ref name=":2"`):

> Separately in the video, Ballard alleges that Kevin Pearson, an LDS area
> president, ordered a Church employee, Kevin Hamilton, to carry out what
> Ballard calls "the hit job" leading to his excommunication.<ref name=":2" />

Wikitext to substitute directly for the broken sentence in "Documentation of
release date" (same location, straight swap, no restructuring):

```
Separately in the video, Ballard alleges that Kevin Pearson, an LDS area president, ordered a Church employee, Kevin Hamilton, to carry out what Ballard calls "the hit job" leading to his excommunication.<ref name=":2" />
```

This keeps a "Kevin" (per the ask — actually keeps both real ones relevant to
this claim, Pearson and Hamilton) and is accurate to what's on tape, unlike
the current text, which garbles "stake president" into "state president" and
attributes the hit job to an unnamed "his state president Kevin" who isn't
actually a stake president at all — Pearson is an Area President, and the
stake president is a *third*, still-unnamed figure in Ballard's account (see
`docs/backfire_state_president_deep_state_claims_2026-09-11.md`).

The McCarthy/McCartney aside isn't part of this fix — different topic
entirely, would need its own context to make sense in an article, not just
folded into the excommunication sentence.
