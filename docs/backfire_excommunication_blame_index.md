# "Backfire" excommunication blame index (running, informal)

Not a formal doc — just a running list of every person/entity Ballard
names or blames in `youtube__pBo_2DYxfrY` ("Backfire: The Excommunication
Story of Tim Ballard," Official Tim Ballard channel, posted 2026-09-04,
3h52m). Keep adding to it as we go through more of the video.

Source: our own Whisper transcript (base model) of all 1053 scene clips
in `dl_wm/outputs/2026-09-07/youtube__pBo_2DYxfrY/scene_clips/`. **Caveat:
Whisper's base model garbles some names/words** — seen so far: "Doug
Anderson" -> "Dijk Anderson" once, "Stake president" -> "Stay president"
repeatedly, a scripture reference to Matthew 5/18 came out mangled as
"B-Matthew V and MF18." Timestamps below are the video's own elapsed
time (h:mm:ss), not clip-relative — good for finding the moment in the
full video or the labeled scene overview.
