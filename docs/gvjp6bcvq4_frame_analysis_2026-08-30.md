  Syria" claim is right, the fakery mechanism is a false location
  claim about real footage, not AI-generated video.

  signature of **recycled/decontextualized footage** — real violence
  from an unrelated time or place, presented as current proof of the
  claimed event. This is a different (and more common, lower-effort)
  disinformation technique than generative fakery, and matches "been
  doing this for decades" — reusing real atrocity footage, not
  fabricating video from nothing.
