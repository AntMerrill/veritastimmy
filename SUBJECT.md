# Subject

**Tim Ballard** — founder and former CEO of Operation Underground Railroad (O.U.R.), a
nonprofit organized around anti-child-trafficking "rescue" operations; former U.S. Department
of Homeland Security agent. Subject of the 2023 film *Sound of Freedom* (Angel Studios) and of
*Hidden War* — per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Ballard).

## Why this repo tracks him

This repo edits the Wikipedia article on him and maintains the legal-exhibit tooling used
against the primary-source litigation material in `tests/inputs/ballard/` (civil suits by
women alleging misconduct, sworn declarations, depositions, expert reports, police reports —
see `tests/inputs/ballard/acj/ACJ_HOLDINGS.md` and `docs/acj_exhibits_inventory.md` for
inventory). The point of the tooling is authenticity verification and accurate sourcing, not
editorializing — see `wiki_replace_edit.py`'s conflict-safe design and the standing rule not to
touch the Suárez v. Ballard conviction detail until the Utah Supreme Court rules.
